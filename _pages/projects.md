## Research Work (In Progress)

I am currently working on several problems in **theoretical cryptography**, focusing on:

- **Distributed Point Functions (DPFs)** – Developing efficient constructions for multi-party settings.  
- **Content Moderation in Decentralized Systems** – Exploring censorship-resistant messaging with confidentiality and deniability guarantees.  
- **Onion Encryption under Honest Moderator Models** – Investigating practical protocols that balance privacy with moderation.

These projects are part of my ongoing PhD research and are in various stages of development.
